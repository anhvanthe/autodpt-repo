## Reading the realistic column

* Energies (E_on, E_off) and switching times are robust: < 2 % / ≈ 1–2 ns with 0.5 % noise.
* **IGBT t_f, t_z, di/dt_off** use the 10 %/2 % current levels, which lie on the slow tail current (≈ 0.07 A/ns): 1 A of noise moves the crossing by ~15 ns. Inherent to the definition; average repetitions (ANA-26) or apply a mild low-pass (ANA-03) for these.
* **SiC I_rrm, Q_rr, E_rec, S**: I_rrm is only 10 % of the current-channel range, so noise biases the peak (max-of-noise). Average repetitions, or measure the diode current on a lower-range probe.
* **Overshoot ΔV** (and absolute peaks) are biased upward by noise (a maximum of a noisy signal); this also applies to the scope's own max measurements.
* De-skew matters more than noise: an uncorrected 4.7 ns current-probe skew changes SiC E_on by −14 % and E_off by +16 % (run `python -m autodpt --sim --device sic --skew 4.7`).
